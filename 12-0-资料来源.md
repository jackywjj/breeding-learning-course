## 01 · 基础、性状与遗传参数 / Foundations, traits, and parameters

### UNE · 数量遗传学 / UNE: quantitative genetics

[数量遗传学课程](https://jvanderw.une.edu.au/325TT)提供遗传作用、遗传力、相关、选择反应和世代间隔的概念框架。  
The [quantitative-genetics course](https://jvanderw.une.edu.au/325TT) provides a framework for genetic components, heritability, correlations, selection response, and generation intervals.

### Purdue／NSIF · 猪遗传改良 / Purdue and NSIF: swine improvement

- [育种与杂交](https://www.extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/NSIF/NSIF-1/NSIF-FS1.html)解释育种与杂交的基础。  
  [Breeding and crossbreeding](https://www.extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/NSIF/NSIF-1/NSIF-FS1.html) explains foundational improvement concepts.

- [遗传参数](https://www.extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/NSIF/NSIF-FS3-W.html)解释遗传力与遗传相关。  
  [Genetic parameters](https://www.extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/NSIF/NSIF-FS3-W.html) explains heritability and genetic correlation.

- [多性状选择](https://www.extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/NSIF/NSIF-10/NSIF-FS10.html)解释多性状目标与限性性状。  
  [Multi-trait selection](https://www.extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/NSIF/NSIF-10/NSIF-FS10.html) discusses multiple objectives and sex-limited traits.

这些资料年代较早，阅读时重点看原理。  
These resources are historical, so focus on their principles.

### AGBU · 猪 EBV / AGBU: pig EBVs

[PIGBLUP EBV 说明](https://agbu.une.edu.au/pig_genetics/ebv.html)介绍猪常见性状、EBV 单位、后代差异及跨场比较条件。  
The [PIGBLUP EBV explanation](https://agbu.une.edu.au/pig_genetics/ebv.html) covers pig traits, EBV units, progeny differences, and across-herd comparison conditions.

## 02 · 统计与系谱 / Statistics and pedigrees

### NIST · 统计定义 / NIST: statistical definitions

- [均值与协方差矩阵](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section5/pmc541.htm)说明均值、方差与协方差。  
  [Means and covariance matrices](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section5/pmc541.htm) explains means, variances, and covariance.

- [相关系数](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/correlat.htm)给出皮尔逊相关的定义。  
  [Correlation](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/correlat.htm) defines Pearson's correlation coefficient.

- [Lew 认证方法与定义](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/strd/univ/certmethdef/lew.html)说明均值、标准差、自相关与数值认证。  
  [Lew certification methods and definitions](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/strd/univ/certmethdef/lew.html) explains means, standard deviations, autocorrelation, and numerical certification.

### University of Missouri Extension · 家系与近交 / Families and inbreeding

- [术语表](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g2030)解释同胞与常见家系术语。  
  The [glossary](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g2030) explains sibling and family terms.

- [近交原理](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g2911)解释关系与近交的基本含义。  
  [Inbreeding principles](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g2911) explains relationships and inbreeding.

本教程统一用 A 元素和 kinship 区分两种常见系数口径。  
This tutorial explicitly distinguishes A-matrix elements from kinship.
