# 07-5｜多性状评估与信息借用 / Multi-trait evaluation and information borrowing

[目录 / Contents](00-目录.md) · [返回主文 / Main chapter](07-0-育种值计算与BLUP.md)

只测了背膘时，我会追踪背膘记录怎样经由协方差影响增重的育种值预测，并用两项都有记录的例子对照。  
When only backfat is measured, I trace how the record can inform daily-gain breeding value through covariance, then compare it with the case where both traits are recorded.

原始来源是 [BREEDPLAN：Multi-Trait Analysis](https://breedplan.une.edu.au/the-breedplan-advantages/the-breedplan-advantage-multi-trait-analysis/)，原对象为肉牛，本篇用原创二性状模型说明通用原理。  
The original source is [BREEDPLAN: Multi-Trait Analysis](https://breedplan.une.edu.au/the-breedplan-advantages/the-breedplan-advantage-multi-trait-analysis/), concerning beef cattle, while this note uses an original two-trait model for general principles.

> **我想弄懂的事｜没有测到目标性状时，其他性状能提供什么信息？**  
> **What I want to understand | What can other traits tell us when the target trait is unmeasured?**

## 01 · 来源要点 / Source points

原页强调相关性状可参与 EBV 预测，并讨论联合分析对管理历史和筛选信息的利用。  
The page emphasizes using related traits in EBV prediction and discusses joint analysis of management history and selection information.

多性状评估还会增加计算复杂度，模型正确性不能只由“联合分析”这个名称保证。  
Multi-trait evaluation also increases computational complexity, and its name alone does not guarantee model correctness.

## 02 · 两种矩阵不要混淆 / Keep the two matrix types separate

动物关系矩阵 A 的行列是动物，而这里的遗传协方差矩阵 $\Sigma_A$ 的行列是性状。  
Rows and columns of relationship matrix A are animals, while those of genetic covariance matrix $\Sigma_A$ here are traits.

假设性状 1 为日增重、性状 2 为背膘，两者加性遗传方差为 100、4，遗传协方差为 −10。  
Suppose Trait 1 is daily gain and Trait 2 backfat, with additive genetic variances 100 and 4 and covariance −10.

$$
\Sigma_A=\begin{bmatrix}100&-10\\-10&4\end{bmatrix},
\quad r_A=-10/\sqrt{100\times4}=-0.5
$$

再假设残差方差为 100、6，残差协方差为 0，且遗传与残差独立。  
Also assume residual variances 100 and 6, zero residual covariance, and independence between genetics and residuals.

$$
\Sigma_e=\begin{bmatrix}100&0\\0&6\end{bmatrix},
\quad V=\Sigma_A+\Sigma_e=\begin{bmatrix}200&-10\\-10&10\end{bmatrix}
$$

## 03 · 只测背膘，预测增重遗传值 / Predict gain genetics using only backfat

假设固定均值和方差已知，一头动物只测得背膘高于基准 2 mm。  
Assume known fixed means and variances and one animal measured only for backfat, two mm above its base.

增重加性作用与背膘记录的协方差为 −10，背膘记录方差为 10。  
Gain's additive effect has covariance −10 with the backfat record, whose variance is 10.

$$
\widehat A_1=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(A_1,P_2)}{\operatorname{Var}(P_2)}(P_2-\mu_2)
=\frac{-10}{10}\times2=-2\ \mathrm{g/day}
$$

这是一项依赖已知协方差的间接预测，不表示背膘本身就是增重育种值。  
This is an indirect prediction relying on known covariance, not a claim that backfat itself is gain breeding value.

## 04 · 两项记录同时存在时 / When both records exist

若同一动物还测得增重高于基准 20 g/day，则偏差向量为 $d=[20,2]'$。  
If the same animal also has gain 20 g/day above base, its deviation vector is $d=[20,2]'$.

目标育种值与两项记录的协方差向量为 $c=[100,-10]$，联合预测为 $cV^{-1}d$。  
Covariance between the target breeding value and both records is $c=[100,-10]$, giving joint prediction $cV^{-1}d$.

对 $2\times2$ 矩阵，逆可通过对角相乘之差作分母、交换对角并改变非对角符号计算。  
For a $2\times2$ matrix, its inverse uses the diagonal-product difference as denominator, swaps diagonal entries, and reverses off-diagonal signs.

$$
V^{-1}=\frac{1}{200\times10-(-10)^2}
\begin{bmatrix}10&10\\10&200\end{bmatrix}
=\frac1{1900}\begin{bmatrix}10&10\\10&200\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
cV^{-1}=[9/19,-10/19],\quad
\widehat A_1=(9/19)20-(10/19)2=160/19\approx8.4211
$$

只用增重自身记录时预测为 $(100/200)20=10$，加入背膘后变为约 8.42，体现新证据的更新而非两个 EBV 的平均。  
Own gain alone predicts $(100/200)20=10$, while adding backfat changes it to about 8.42, an evidence update rather than an average of two EBVs.

## 05 · 信息增加与预测值升降不同 / More information need not increase the EBV

在这个正确且方差已知的模型中，单记录 PEV 为 $100-100^2/200=50$。  
In this correct known-variance model, the single-record PEV is $100-100^2/200=50$.

两项联合的 PEV 为 $100-cV^{-1}c'=900/19\approx47.3684$，不确定性减少，但 EBV 数值下降。  
Joint PEV is $100-cV^{-1}c'=900/19\approx47.3684$, so uncertainty decreases while the EBV decreases.

信息更充分不等于遗传水平必然更高，而是预测更能反映数据提供的方向。  
Better information does not necessarily raise predicted genetic merit but allows the prediction to reflect the evidence.

## 06 · 缺失值和选择过程 / Missingness and selection

没测背膘不能填成背膘偏差 0，因为未观察不等于恰好达到基准。  
Unmeasured backfat cannot be filled with deviation zero because unobserved does not mean exactly at the base.

若只有高增重猪才继续测背膘，测定选择机制应尽可能进入联合信息，而不能假定记录随机缺失。  
If only high-gain pigs receive backfat measurements, the selection process should be represented in the joint information rather than assuming random missingness.

联合模型有助于利用相关信息，但不能自动修复未记录的筛选原因或错误身份。  
Joint models help use correlated information but do not automatically repair unrecorded selection reasons or incorrect identities.

## 07 · 评估与综合指数不是一件事 / Evaluation is not an economic index

多性状评估先预测各性状遗传值，综合指数再按育种目标组合这些预测。  
Multi-trait evaluation predicts genetic merit for each trait, while a selection index combines predictions according to the breeding objective.

遗传协方差用于描述生物学信息联系，经济权重用于描述目标价值，不能互相替代。  
Genetic covariance describes biological information links, while economic weights express objective value, and they are not interchangeable.

## 08 · 检查模型是否真的联合预测 / Check Whether the Model Uses Joint Prediction

我会看模型是否把遗传协方差、残差协方差和共享记录信息放进同一次预测，再把它与两列单性状 EBV 的平均区分开。  
I check whether the model uses genetic and residual covariances and shared record information within a joint prediction, then distinguish it from an average of two single-trait EBVs.
