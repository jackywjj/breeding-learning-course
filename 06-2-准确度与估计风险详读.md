# 06-2｜准确度与估计风险 / Accuracy and estimation risk

[目录 / Contents](00-目录.md) · [返回主文 / Main chapter](06-0-育种值与准确度.md)

如果要在两头猪之间做决定，我会把 EBV 与 ACC 放在不同列，先对齐性状与基准，再比较预测值和不确定性。  
For a choice between two pigs, I keep EBV and ACC in separate columns, align the trait and base, and then compare the estimates and their uncertainty.

> **我想弄懂的事｜EBV 更高和估计更稳，怎样同时理解？**  
> **What I want to understand | How can higher merit and greater certainty be interpreted together?**

## 01 · 来源要点 / Source points

原页把准确度与可用信息和新增数据后的变化风险联系起来，同时建议按 EBV 比较遗传水平。  
The page links accuracy with available information and revision risk while recommending EBVs for comparing genetic merit.

原文讨论“spreading-the-risk”，但实际分散程度取决于动物关系与预测误差是否相关。  
The source discusses “spreading-the-risk,” whose actual benefit depends on animal relationships and correlated prediction errors.

## 02 · ACC 与 REL 不是同一个数字 / ACC and REL are different quantities

理论**准确度 ACC**是预测值与真实育种值的相关，**可靠性 REL**通常定义为准确度平方。  
Theoretical **accuracy ACC** is the correlation between prediction and true breeding value, while **reliability REL** is usually its square.

$$
REL=ACC^2,\qquad ACC=\sqrt{REL}
$$

ACC 为 0.8 时 REL 为 0.64，若改用百分数显示就是 80% 与 64%。  
ACC 0.8 gives REL 0.64, displayed as 80% and 64% on percentage scales.

80% 不是“有 80% 概率这个 EBV 完全正确”，也不是每个后代都能兑现 80% 优势。  
The 80% is not an 80% probability of an exactly correct EBV or a guarantee that every offspring realizes 80% of the advantage.

## 03 · 用 PEV 看单位上的不确定性 / Express uncertainty in trait units

**PEV**是模型下预测误差的方差，开平方得到相应的预测误差标准差。  
**PEV** is model-based prediction error variance, whose square root gives prediction error standard deviation.

假设某不近交动物的遗传方差为 400 $(g/day)^2$、PEV 为 144 $(g/day)^2$。  
Suppose a non-inbred animal has genetic variance 400 $(g/day)^2$ and PEV 144 $(g/day)^2$.

$$
REL=1-144/400=0.64,\qquad ACC=0.8,\qquad SEP=\sqrt{144}=12\ \mathrm{g/day}
$$

这里 **SEP** 表示预测误差标准差，不是第 03 章用 $s/\sqrt n$ 估计的独立样本均值标准误。  
Here **SEP** denotes prediction error standard deviation, not the independent-sample mean standard error estimated by $s/\sqrt n$ in Chapter 03.

在固定条件和方差已知的正态条件预测示例中，若 EBV 为 +30，可用 $30\pm1.96\times12$ 表示约 95% 的条件预测区间。  
In a normal conditional-prediction example with known fixed conditions and variances, an EBV of +30 has an approximate 95% conditional prediction interval $30\pm1.96\times12$.

$$
[30-23.52,30+23.52]=[6.48,53.52]\ \mathrm{g/day}
$$

这是指定概率模型下对潜在 BV 的预测区间，不能与所有频率学派参数置信区间混用，也不能忽略方差估计和模型错误。  
This is a prediction interval for latent BV under a specified probability model, not interchangeable with every frequentist parameter interval or immune to variance-estimation and model error.

## 04 · 为什么不能把 ACC 再乘到 EBV 上 / Why not multiply EBV by ACC again?

假设公猪 A 的 EBV 为 +40、ACC 为 0.6，B 的 EBV 为 +30、ACC 为 0.9。  
Suppose Boar A has EBV +40 and ACC 0.6, while B has EBV +30 and ACC 0.9.

EBV 是模型当前对遗传水平的预测，A 的预测更高，而 B 的估计更有把握。  
EBV is the current prediction of genetic merit, so A has the higher predicted level while B is more certain.

若再相乘，会算出 A 为 24、B 为 27，但这个新排序不是原评估的最佳线性预测。  
Multiplying again gives A 24 and B 27, but this new ranking is not the original evaluation's best linear prediction.

信息量不足带来的收缩通常已经进入 EBV，风险偏好应作为决策问题另外表达。  
Shrinkage from limited information is usually already incorporated in EBV, while risk preference should be expressed separately as a decision issue.

## 05 · 信息多不等于全都独立有效 / More records are not always independent information

自身记录、父母信息、后代记录和相关性状可以提高信息量，但效果依赖遗传力、同期群与模型结构。  
Own, parental, progeny, and correlated-trait records can add information, with their value depending on heritability, contemporary groups, and model structure.

把同一测量复制十次不会产生十次独立证据，同一家系的大量记录也可能高度共享信息。  
Copying one measurement ten times adds no tenfold independent evidence, and many records in one family can share substantial information.

对多头公猪的平均预测误差，方差不仅含各自的 PEV，还含公猪之间的预测误差协方差。  
Prediction error variance of a boar-team mean includes both individual PEVs and between-boar prediction error covariances.

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\bar\varepsilon)=\frac{1}{k^2}
\left[\sum_i PEV_i+2\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon_i,\varepsilon_j)\right]
$$

因此“用多头公猪”可以分散部分风险，但不能把近亲公猪简单当作完全独立的投资组合。  
Using multiple boars can spread some risk but does not make closely related boars a fully independent portfolio.

## 06 · 把遗传价值与估计把握度分两列看 / Read Merit and Certainty in Separate Columns

我用可比基准下的 EBV 和育种目标判断遗传价值，再用 ACC 看这个估计有多少信息支持。  
I judge genetic merit using comparable EBVs and the breeding objective, then use ACC to see how well the estimate is supported.

两头动物可以是一头 EBV 更高、另一头 ACC 更高，选谁要结合目标和愿意承担的不确定性。  
One animal can have the higher EBV and the other the higher ACC, so the choice depends on the objective and the uncertainty one is willing to accept.
