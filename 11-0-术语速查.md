## 01 · 基础概念 / Foundations

- **性状 Trait**是描述或测量的生物特征，例如日增重。  
  A **trait** is a biological characteristic that can be described or measured, such as daily gain.

- **表型 Phenotype**是实际观测结果，例如 850 g/day。  
  A **phenotype** is an observed outcome, such as 850 g/day.

- **基因型 Genotype**是动物的遗传构成。  
  A **genotype** is an animal's genetic constitution.

- **加性作用 Additive effects**描述平均等位基因替换效应。  
  **Additive effects** describe average allele-substitution effects.

- **显性 Dominance**涉及同一位点的拷贝组合。  
  **Dominance** concerns combinations of copies at one locus.

- **上位互作 Epistasis**涉及不同位点的互作。  
  **Epistasis** concerns interactions across loci.

## 02 · 育种值与不确定性 / Breeding values and uncertainty

- **BV**是相对参考群体的加性遗传价值。  
  **BV** is additive genetic merit relative to a reference population.

- **EBV**是对育种值的预测。  
  **EBV** is an estimated breeding value.

- **GEBV**是利用基因组信息得到的育种值预测。  
  **GEBV** is a genomic estimated breeding value.

- **EPD**是预期后代差异，常见尺度为 EBV 的一半。  
  **EPD** is expected progeny difference, commonly on half the EBV scale.

- **PA**是亲本均值，即父母育种值的平均。  
  **PA** is parent average, the mean of parental breeding values.

- **MS**是孟德尔抽样，即子代真实育种值相对真实亲本均值的遗传偏离。  
  **MS** is Mendelian sampling, an offspring's true genetic deviation from the true parent average.

- **ACC**是准确度，描述预测与真实育种值的相关程度。  
  **ACC** is accuracy, describing correlation between predicted and true breeding values.

- **REL**是可靠性，常用定义为 $REL=ACC^2$。  
  **REL** is reliability, commonly defined as $REL=ACC^2$.

- **PEV**是预测误差方差，衡量预测误差的变异。  
  **PEV** is prediction error variance, measuring variation in prediction error.

---

## 03 · 参数与家系 / Parameters and families

- **遗传力 Heritability**描述群体表型变异中的加性遗传比例。  
  **Heritability** describes the additive genetic share of population phenotypic variation.

- **重复力 Repeatability**描述同一动物重复记录的一致程度。  
  **Repeatability** describes consistency across an animal's repeated records.

- **遗传相关 Genetic correlation**描述两个性状加性遗传作用的共同变化。  
  **Genetic correlation** describes co-variation in additive effects for two traits.

- **家系 Family**是按指定亲缘关系组成的动物群。  
  A **family** groups animals by a specified relationship.

- **系谱 Pedigree**是动物祖先与父母的记录。  
  A **pedigree** records ancestry and parentage.

- **Sire**是父亲，**Dam**是母亲。  
  **Sire** means father, and **dam** means mother.

- **Progeny**是后代。  
  **Progeny** means offspring.

- **Full siblings**是同父同母的全同胞。  
  **Full siblings** share both parents.

- **Half siblings**是只共享一位父母的半同胞。  
  **Half siblings** share one parent.

- **Relationship**是动物之间的遗传联系。  
  **Relationship** describes genetic connections between animals.

- **Inbreeding**涉及同祖同源的等位基因拷贝在个体内相遇。  
  **Inbreeding** concerns identical-by-descent allele copies meeting within an individual.

---

## 04 · 模型与矩阵 / Models and matrices

- **A、G、H**在本教程分别是系谱、基因组、组合关系矩阵。  
  **A, G, H** denote pedigree, genomic, and combined relationship matrices in this tutorial.

- 某些统计教材用 **G** 表示全部随机效应的协方差矩阵，需要按上下文辨认。  
  Some statistical texts use **G** for the full random-effect covariance matrix, so read the context.

- **BLUP**是最佳线性无偏预测。  
  **BLUP** means best linear unbiased prediction.

- **REML**是限制最大似然，可用于估计方差组分。  
  **REML** means restricted maximum likelihood and can estimate variance components.

- **MME**是混合模型方程。  
  **MME** means mixed model equations.

- **固定效应 Fixed effect**估计指定水平的差异。  
  A **fixed effect** estimates differences among specified levels.

- **随机效应 Random effect**按分布与协方差结构建模。  
  A **random effect** is modeled through a distribution and covariance structure.

- **协变量 Covariate**是进入模型的解释变量，例如日龄。  
  A **covariate** is an explanatory variable entering a model, such as age.

- **CG**是同期群，用于组织表现机会相近的动物进行比较。  
  **CG** means contemporary group, used to compare animals with similar performance opportunities.

- **PE**是永久环境，持续影响同一动物的多次记录。  
  **PE** means permanent environment, persisting across an animal's records.

- **Parity**是胎次，表示第几次分娩。  
  **Parity** identifies the farrowing number.

## 05 · 性状与记录缩写 / Traits and recording abbreviations

- **直接遗传 Direct genetic**指被测动物自身的遗传作用。  
  **Direct genetic** refers to the measured animal's own genetic effects.

- **母体遗传 Maternal genetic**指母亲的遗传组成影响后代表现。  
  **Maternal genetic** refers to the mother's genetics affecting offspring performance.

- **限性 Sex-limited**指性状只在某一性别表现。  
  **Sex-limited** means a trait is expressed in one sex.

- **伴性 Sex-linked**指相关位点在性染色体上。  
  **Sex-linked** refers to loci on sex chromosomes.

- **ADG**是平均日增重。  
  **ADG** means average daily gain.

- **ADFI**是平均日采食量。  
  **ADFI** means average daily feed intake.

- **FCR**是料重比，即采食量除以增重。  
  **FCR** is feed conversion ratio, or feed consumed divided by weight gained.

- **RFI**是剩余采食量，即实际采食量与指定模型预测值的偏差。  
  **RFI** is residual feed intake, the deviation of actual intake from a specified model's prediction.

- **TNB**是总产仔数。  
  **TNB** means total number born.

- **NBA**是产活仔数。  
  **NBA** means number born alive.

- **NW**是断奶仔猪数。  
  **NW** means number weaned.

- **BF**是背膘厚。  
  **BF** means backfat.

- **IMF**是肌内脂肪。  
  **IMF** means intramuscular fat.

实际系统的缩写与口径必须另外确认。  
Verify abbreviations and definitions in the actual data system.

## 06 · 育种决策 / Breeding decisions

- **选择强度 Selection intensity**描述选择的严格程度。  
  **Selection intensity** describes the stringency of selection.

- **世代间隔 Generation interval**是替代后代出生时父母的平均年龄。  
  **Generation interval** is the average parental age at birth of replacement offspring.

- **杂种优势 Heterosis**是杂交群体相对亲本群体平均的优势。  
  **Heterosis** is crossbred superiority relative to the parental-population average.
