# 05-3｜动物模型与系谱信息 / Animal models and pedigree information

I trace predictions for three animals back to a single observation to see where parents without own records begin to gain information.

> **我想弄懂的事｜祖先没有测定记录，为什么仍能获得预测？**  
> **What I want to understand | Why can an ancestor without a measurement still receive a prediction?**

## 01 · 来源要点 / Source points

原文“animal model”指包含个体加性遗传效应，并用亲缘矩阵联系动物的混合模型。  
The source term “animal model” refers to a mixed model with individual additive genetic effects linked by a relationship matrix.

作者分别介绍观测文件、参数文件和系谱文件，并指出结果文件混合了固定与随机效应解。  
The author distinguishes observation, parameter, and pedigree files and notes that solutions contain both fixed and random effects.

以下矩阵、编号和计算与本教程第 07 章一致，属于自拟的已知均值特例。  
The matrices, IDs, and calculations below match Chapter 07 and form an original known-mean teaching case.

## 02 · 一条记录与三头动物 / One record and three animals

设父母 S、D 无关且不近交，C 为子代，只有 C 测得日增重 850 g/day。  
Let unrelated, non-inbred parents S and D have offspring C, with only C measured at 850 g/day.

额外假设总体均值已知为 800，遗传与残差方差分别为 300、700 $(g/day)^2$，并无其他待估效应。  
Additionally assume a known population mean of 800, genetic and residual variances 300 and 700 $(g/day)^2$, and no other effects to estimate.

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0.5\\0&1&0.5\\0.5&0.5&1\end{bmatrix},
\qquad Z=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&1\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Z** 是记录到动物的映射，唯一一行只指向 C，但 **A** 包含三个动物的关系。  
**Z** maps records to animals, with its single row pointing only to C, while **A** contains relationships among all three animals.

## 03 · 用协方差传递信息 / Information travels through covariance

动物加性遗传协方差为 $300A$，所以 S 与 C、D 与 C 的遗传协方差各为 150。  
Additive genetic covariance is $300A$, giving genetic covariance 150 between S and C and between D and C.

C 的观测偏差为 $850-800=50$，观测方差为 $300+700=1000$。  
C's observed deviation is $850-800=50$, and observation variance is $300+700=1000$.

$$
\widehat a_S=\frac{150}{1000}\times50=7.5,
\quad\widehat a_D=7.5,
\quad\widehat a_C=\frac{300}{1000}\times50=15
$$

S、D 的预测来自子代信息与亲缘结构，不需要凭空生成它们的自身日增重。  
S and D are predicted through offspring information and relationships without inventing their own gain measurements.

这三项预测来自同一条观测，不能再当成三条独立证据相加。  
The three predictions derive from one observation and must not be added as three independent pieces of evidence.

## 04 · 误差方差也随关系变化 / Error variances also depend on relationships

在这个已知均值的特例中，预测误差方差等于原遗传方差减去观测减少的不确定性。  
In this known-mean case, prediction error variance is the original genetic variance minus uncertainty removed by the observation.

$$
PEV_C=300-300^2/1000=210
$$

$$
PEV_S=PEV_D=300-150^2/1000=277.5
$$

因此 C 的可靠性为 $1-210/300=0.30$，而父母各为 $1-277.5/300=0.075$。  
Thus C has reliability $1-210/300=0.30$, while each parent has $1-277.5/300=0.075$.

没有自身记录不等于没有信息，但通过较远关系获得的证据通常弱于直接观测。  
No own record does not mean no information, but information through relationships is usually weaker than direct observation.

## 05 · 加入未知固定效应后 / When fixed effects must be estimated

现实中常写为 $y=Xb+Za+e$，其中 $b$ 可能包含批次、性别、胎次和日龄等项。  
Real models often use $y=Xb+Za+e$, with $b$ including batch, sex, parity, or age terms.

**固定效应**估计指定条件差异，**随机效应**则带有群体方差与协方差结构。  
**Fixed effects** estimate specified condition differences, while **random effects** have population variance and covariance structures.

均值未知时必须与遗传效应一起处理，不能把上面已知均值的分数公式当成完整生产流程。  
An unknown mean must be handled together with genetic effects, so the fractions above are not a complete production pipeline.

具体方程演算见 [混合模型方程逐步推导](07-2-混合模型方程逐步推导详读.md)。  
See [mixed model equations step by step](07-2-混合模型方程逐步推导详读.md) for the full calculation.

## 06 · 如何读结果文件 / Read a solutions file

`trait` 指性状，`effect` 指效应项，`level` 指该效应的水平或动物编号，`solution` 是对应解。  
`trait` identifies a trait, `effect` a model term, `level` its category or animal ID, and `solution` its estimate or prediction.

只有对应加性遗传效应的行才能解释成该模型的 EBV，场区解与日龄回归系数都不是动物 EBV。  
Only rows for additive genetic effects represent EBVs in that model, while site solutions and age-regression coefficients do not.

原始编号与重编号之间应保留映射，不能把某一效应的水平 10 当作任何表中的第十头猪。  
Preserve original-to-renumbered ID mappings rather than treating level 10 of any effect as the tenth pig in an arbitrary table.

## 07 · 祖先有 EBV，回查它的数据来源 / Trace an Ancestor’s EBV Back to the Data

看到没有明显记录的祖先也有非零 EBV，我会回查自测表型、亲属和后代记录分别有没有进入评估。  
When an ancestor without an obvious measurement has a nonzero EBV, I check whether its own phenotypes, relatives’ records, and progeny records entered the evaluation.

亲属和后代能通过关系结构影响祖先的预测，EBV 有数值只说明模型给出了预测。  
Relatives and progeny can influence an ancestor’s prediction through the relationship structure, and a reported EBV shows that the model made a prediction.
