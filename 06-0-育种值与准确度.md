# 06-0｜育种值与准确度 / Breeding values and accuracy

[目录 / Contents](00-目录.md) · [上一章 / Previous](05-0-家系系谱与近交.md) · [下一章 / Next](07-0-育种值计算与BLUP.md)

拿到一张育种值表，我先不急着排序，得知道一个 +30 在哪一列、用什么单位、相对于哪一批动物。  
When I read a breeding-value table, I hold off on sorting until I know which column a +30 belongs to, its unit, and its reference population.

我的读表顺序是先把表现、真实育种值、估计育种值和预期后代差异分清，再看准确度这列怎么定义。  
My reading order is to separate performance, true breeding value, estimated breeding value, and expected progeny difference, then check the definition of the accuracy field.

> **我的笔记｜EBV 描述遗传价值，准确度描述估计把握。**  
> **My note | EBV describes genetic merit, while accuracy describes confidence in that estimate.**

## 01 · BV 和 EBV 是什么 / What BV and EBV mean

**育种值 BV**是动物对指定性状的加性遗传价值，按参考群体定义。  
A **breeding value (BV)** is additive genetic merit for a specified trait, defined relative to a reference population.

真实 BV 通常不能直接观察。  
True BV is usually unobservable.

**估计育种值 EBV**利用自身、亲属及可能的基因组信息来预测 BV。  
An **estimated breeding value (EBV)** predicts BV using own, relative, and possibly genomic information.

BV 和 EBV 都不是对动物未来实际表现的保证。  
Neither BV nor EBV guarantees future actual performance.

### 用计算机理解 / A computing analogy

- **表型**像受运行环境影响的观测输出。  
  **Phenotype** resembles observed output affected by the runtime environment.

- **BV**像希望识别的潜在遗传信号。  
  **BV** resembles the latent genetic signal of interest.

- **EBV**像模型对这个信号的估计。  
  **EBV** resembles the model's estimate of that signal.

这个类比只区分观测与潜在量，不意味着 DNA 是确定性程序。  
This analogy distinguishes observed and latent quantities without implying that DNA is a deterministic program.

## 02 · 先读单位和基准 / Read units and the base first

假设 ADG EBV 为 **+30 g/day**，它表示相对评估基准的加性遗传优势。  
A hypothetical ADG EBV of **+30 g/day** indicates an additive advantage relative to the evaluation base.

它不表示实际日增重只有 30 g/day。  
It does not mean actual daily gain is only 30 g/day.

EBV 为零表示处于该基准，不表示没有遗传价值。  
An EBV of zero denotes the reference base, not absence of genetic merit.

在 FCR 或达重日龄等越小越好的性状上，负 EBV 可能有利。  
Negative EBVs can be favorable for traits such as FCR or days to target weight.

读一个 EBV，至少确认以下六项。  
Confirm at least the following six items when interpreting an EBV.

- **性状定义**决定它在比较什么。  
  The **trait definition** establishes what is being compared.

- **单位**决定数值如何解释。  
  **Units** establish how to interpret the number.

- **基准**决定零点在哪里。  
  The **base** establishes the reference zero.

- **评估群体**决定比较对象。  
  The **evaluation population** establishes the comparison group.

- **版本或日期**决定使用哪次评估。  
  The **version or date** identifies the evaluation used.

- **准确度**说明估计的不确定程度。  
  **Accuracy** describes uncertainty in the estimate.

不同场、品种或系统的 EBV，直接比较需要共同评估或经过验证的可比标定。  
Direct comparison across farms, breeds, or systems requires a shared evaluation or validated calibration.

跨场比较还需要足够的遗传联系。  
Across-farm comparison also requires sufficient genetic connectedness.

## 03 · 后代差异为什么通常除以 2 / Why progeny differences use one-half

假设同一评估中，两头公猪的 ADG EBV 分别为 +40 和 +10 g/day。  
Suppose two boars in the same evaluation have ADG EBVs of +40 and +10 g/day.

如果母猪群遗传水平相当、后代环境相当，后代群体期望差异为 **15 g/day**。  
With genetically equivalent mates and comparable offspring environments, the expected progeny-group difference is **15 g/day**.

计算为 $(40-10)/2=15$，因为父母各贡献一半常染色体遗传信息。  
The calculation is $(40-10)/2=15$, because each parent contributes half the autosomal genetic material.

**EPD**是预期后代差异，常见口径约为 $EBV/2$。  
An **expected progeny difference (EPD)** is commonly on the $EBV/2$ scale.

如果字段已经是 EPD，核对口径后不要再除以 2。  
If a field already reports EPD, check its definition and do not halve it again.

个体后代仍会因孟德尔抽样和环境而偏离群体期望。  
Individual offspring still deviate from group expectations through Mendelian sampling and environment.

### 亲本均值与孟德尔抽样 / Parent average and Mendelian sampling

真实子代育种值等于“父母真实育种值的平均 + 孟德尔抽样偏离”。  
An offspring's true breeding value equals “the mean of the parents' true breeding values + Mendelian-sampling deviation.”

$$
BV_C=\tfrac12 BV_S+\tfrac12 BV_D+MS_C,\qquad
E(MS_C\mid\text{parents})=0
$$

假设父母真实 BV 为 +40 和 +20，子代条件期望为 +30，但各个同胞仍可不同。  
If the parents' true BVs are +40 and +20, the conditional offspring expectation is +30, while individual siblings can differ.

这里描述的是加性遗传值，不包含全部环境和杂种优势。  
This describes additive genetic merit, not the complete effects of environment and heterosis.

实际不知道真实 BV 时，父母 EBV 的平均可作为子代初步预测。  
When true BVs are unknown, the mean of parental EBVs can provide an initial offspring prediction.

自身、同胞、后代或基因组信息可以进一步更新预测。  
Own, sibling, progeny, or genomic information can update that prediction.

父母 EBV 仍是估计，需要结合准确度、共同信息和基准理解。  
Parental EBVs remain estimates whose accuracies, shared information, and bases must be considered.

## 04 · ACC、REL 与 PEV / Accuracy, reliability, and PEV

- **准确度 ACC**描述预测育种值与真实育种值的相关程度。  
  **Accuracy (ACC)** describes the correlation between predicted and true breeding value.

- **可靠性 REL**通常是准确度的平方，即 $REL=ACC^2$。  
  **Reliability (REL)** is usually squared accuracy, or $REL=ACC^2$.

- **预测误差方差 PEV**衡量 $\widehat{BV}-BV$ 的误差变异。  
  **Prediction error variance (PEV)** measures variation in $\widehat{BV}-BV$.

假设 ACC = 0.80，则 REL = 0.64。  
If ACC = 0.80, then REL = 0.64.

这不是“有 80% 概率该 EBV 完全正确”。  
This is not an 80% probability that the EBV is exactly correct.

在方差已知、基准一致的标准模型下，常用 $REL_i=1-PEV_i/[(1+F_i)\sigma_A^2]$。  
Under a standard model with known variances and a consistent base, a common expression is $REL_i=1-PEV_i/[(1+F_i)\sigma_A^2]$.

实际系统可能采用近似或不同定义，必须核对字段口径。  
Actual systems may use approximations or different conventions, so check field definitions.

仅凭一个 EBV 数字，不能反推出 ACC。  
An EBV value alone cannot determine ACC.

---

我读教学报表时会先圈出性状、基准和日期，再把估值与把握程度拆开。  
In a teaching report, I mark the trait, base, and date first, then read the estimate and its certainty separately.

例如同次评估中有两行日增重 EBV，先约定单位都是 g/day、基准相同、准确度都采用 ACC 口径。  
For two daily-gain EBV rows in the same evaluation, suppose both use g/day, share a base, and report accuracy as ACC.

| 动物 / Animal | 日增重 EBV / Daily-gain EBV (g/day) | 准确度 / ACC | 可靠性 / REL |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 甲 / A | +50 | 0.70 | 0.49 |
| 乙 / B | +10 | 0.80 | 0.64 |

在这张表里，甲的估计遗传水平更高，乙的估计相对更有把握。  
In this table, A has the higher estimated genetic merit and B has the more certain estimate.

把 ACC 平方就能得到右边的 REL，甲的 0.49 和乙的 0.64 都来自同一份评估。  
Squaring ACC gives the REL in the right-hand column, with A’s 0.49 and B’s 0.64 both coming from the same evaluation.

## 05 · 价值高，不等于把握大 / High merit is not high certainty

**高 EBV、低 ACC**可能有潜力，但未来更新幅度也可能较大。  
**High EBV with low ACC** may be promising but subject to larger future revisions.

**较低 EBV、高 ACC**说明信息较充分，却不自动代表遗传价值更高。  
**Lower EBV with high ACC** is better supported but not automatically genetically superior.

不要把 ACC 再乘到 EBV 上当作折扣，因为信息不足带来的收缩通常已在评估中体现。  
Do not multiply EBV by ACC as a discount, because the evaluation usually already accounts for limited information through shrinkage.

估计不确定性带来的决策风险需要另行考虑。  
Decision risk from estimation uncertainty needs separate consideration.

## 我的核对笔记 / My Verification Notes

换一组容易核对的数，在同一基准下两头公猪的日增重 EBV 为 +50 和 +10 g/day。  
For another easy-to-check example, two boars on the same base have daily-gain EBVs of +50 and +10 g/day.

若与遗传水平相当的母猪群配对且后代环境可比，后代群均值的期望差是 $(50-10)/2=20$ g/day，已用 EPD 尺度报告时只需比较两项 EPD。  
With genetically equivalent mates and comparable offspring environments, the expected progeny-group difference is $(50-10)/2=20$ g/day, and if both are already reported as EPDs, compare those two EPDs directly.

准确度为 0.70 时，可靠性是 $0.70^2=0.49$，但单看 EBV 为 +20 无法反推出准确度。  
An accuracy of 0.70 gives reliability $0.70^2=0.49$, and an EBV of +20 alone does not reveal the accuracy.
