## 01 · 来源要点 / Source points

原文反复强调“reference population”，以及基因组信息不能替代表型记录。  
The source repeatedly emphasizes the “reference population” and that genomic information does not replace performance recording.

文章用青年牛和全同胞举例，说明基因型可在自身记录有限时增加信息，但收益依赖参考群体与性状。  
The article uses young cattle and full siblings to illustrate information gains with limited own records, subject to the reference population and trait.

其参考群体规模示例来自当时肉牛条件，本篇不把它变成种猪必须达到的固定样本门槛。  
Its reference-size examples reflect historical beef conditions and are not treated as a fixed required sample threshold for pigs.

## 02 · 三个角色 / Three roles

- **参考群体**为模型提供基因型与可用表型之间的联系，通常还需要系谱与管理信息。  
  The **reference population** provides connections between genotypes and usable phenotypes, usually alongside pedigree and management information.

- **候选群体**是需要预测和选择的动物，其中一些尚无自身性状记录。  
  The **candidate population** contains animals requiring prediction and selection, some without own trait records.

- **验证群体**用于检查预测能否应用到指定对象，不能同时随意作为已经看过答案的训练集。  
  The **validation population** checks prediction for the intended target and must not casually be treated as training data whose answers were already seen.

这些角色取决于时间和分析设计，同一动物的记录不能在同一评估中既当隐藏答案又泄漏进训练。  
These roles depend on timing and design, and an animal's withheld answer must not leak into training within the same evaluation.

## 03 · 为什么全同胞可以被区分 / Why full siblings can be distinguished

假设两头同胞 S1、S2 的父母 EBV 为 +40、+20，则无其他区分信息时亲本均值都是 +30。  
Suppose full siblings S1 and S2 have parental EBVs +40 and +20, giving both parent averages +30 without other distinguishing information.

分型显示二者继承组合不同，若参考群体提供相关证据，更新后的预测可以不同。  
Genotyping can reveal different inherited combinations, allowing different updated predictions when reference data provide relevant evidence.

例如自拟更新结果为 +34 与 +27，仅表示模型加入信息后作出区分，不能说芯片直接测出了真实 BV。  
Hypothetical updated predictions of +34 and +27 show model-based differentiation, not direct chip measurement of true BV.

没有适用参考信息时，检测到 SNP 差异本身不告诉你哪个差异对目标性状更有利。  
Without applicable reference information, detecting SNP differences alone does not establish which differences favor the target trait.

## 04 · 影响效果的五个条件 / Five conditions affecting performance

- **记录质量**决定表型标签是否可用，错误猪号会把基因型和错误表现连接。  
  **Record quality** determines phenotype usability, with wrong IDs connecting genotypes to the wrong outcomes.

- **参考代表性**决定目标品种、血缘和环境是否得到覆盖。  
  **Reference representativeness** determines coverage of target breeds, relationships, and environments.

- **样本量与有效信息**同时重要，许多重复或高度冗余记录不等于同量独立证据。  
  **Sample size and effective information** both matter, as many duplicated or redundant records do not equal the same amount of independent evidence.

- **性状定义**必须一致，别把日增重、达重日龄或料重比混成同一标签。  
  **Trait definition** must be consistent rather than mixing daily gain, days to weight, and FCR into one label.

- **持续更新**把新世代的真实记录带回参考群体，避免只依赖越来越远的旧数据。  
  **Continual updating** adds new-generation records rather than relying only on increasingly distant historical data.

## 05 · 为什么随机拆分有时过于乐观 / Why random splits can be optimistic

若训练集与验证集含许多近亲，随机拆分可能反映对近亲的预测能力，却不代表对陌生品种或未来条件的能力。  
With many close relatives across training and validation, random splitting may measure prediction of relatives without establishing transfer to unrelated breeds or future conditions.

这不表示所有亲属拆分都错误，而是验证对象必须与真正使用场景一致。  
This does not make every relatives-based split wrong, but validation targets must match intended use.

一种自拟时间验证是只用截止日已知记录预测下一批青年猪，等未来表现出现后再评分。  
One original time-validation design predicts the next young cohort using only information available at cutoff and scores predictions after later outcomes arrive.

候选的未来表型、由未来表型更新的亲属 EBV 和使用全数据计算的筛选变量都可能泄漏答案。  
Future candidate phenotypes, relatives' EBVs updated with future phenotypes, and selection variables computed from all data can leak answers.

## 06 · 验证相关不是自动等于 ACC / Validation correlation is not automatically ACC

假设三头动物的预测为 $[10,20,30]$，未来校正表现为 $[14,16,24]$。  
Suppose three animals have predictions $[10,20,30]$ and later adjusted performances $[14,16,24]$.

两列均值分别为 20、18，交叉离差和为 100，平方和为 200、56。  
Their means are 20 and 18, cross-deviation sum is 100, and squared sums are 200 and 56.

$$
r=100/\sqrt{200\times56}\approx0.9449
$$

这个很小的教学样本只演示运算，算出的是与未来表型的相关，不是与不可直接观测真实 BV 的相关。  
This tiny sample only demonstrates arithmetic, calculating correlation with future phenotype rather than unobservable true BV.

还应看样本规模、误差范围、均值偏差、预测尺度和目标群体，不能凭一个高相关宣布评估准确。  
Sample size, uncertainty, mean bias, prediction scale, and target population also matter, so one high correlation cannot certify accurate evaluation.

## 07 · 用验证数据衡量升级收益 / Measure the Benefit with Validation Data

要判断更密的芯片是否提高了预测，我会在目标群体上比较旧方案和新方案，用同一套验证设计。  
To see whether a denser chip improves prediction, I compare the old and new approaches on the target population with the same validation design.

样本身份、参考群体、表型质量和群体适用性都可能限制收益，这些条件要一起核对。  
Sample identity, reference data, phenotype quality, and population relevance can limit the benefit and should all be checked.
