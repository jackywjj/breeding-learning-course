09-3｜同期群：先确认谁有资格直接比较 / Contemporary groups: who can be directly compared?

目录 / Contents · 第09章 / Chapter 09 · 上一篇 / Previous · 下一篇 / Next

我会看一条记录经过哪些饲养和测定事件，再判断哪些动物适合放在一起比较。
I trace the management and testing events behind a record before deciding which animals belong in the same comparison.

我想弄懂的事｜两头猪都长了 900 g/day，是否就能说遗传水平一样？
What I want to understand | If two pigs both gain 900 g/day, do they have equal genetic merit?

主来源：Understanding BREEDPLAN Contemporary Groups，核对日期为 2026-10-17。
Principal source: Understanding BREEDPLAN Contemporary Groups, checked on 2026-10-17.

原文针对肉牛，本篇将其原则与种猪教学例子分开说明。
The source concerns beef cattle, and this note separates its principles from pig teaching examples.

01 · 原文短摘与要点 / Short excerpt and source summary

短摘译意：有相同的表现机会。
Short excerpt: “an equal opportunity to perform”.

这是原文开头解释同期群时的核心措辞。
This is the central wording used in the source’s opening explanation.

原文只把表现机会相近的动物放在同组直接比较，并提醒分组错误会损害评估。
The source directly compares animals with similar opportunities and warns that incorrect grouping harms evaluation.

它既反对把不同待遇混在一起，也指出过小分组会削弱比较信息。
It discourages mixing different treatments and notes that overly small groups weaken comparisons.

所列分组依据包括场、出生年份、性别、出生类型、母龄、品种、测量日期、日龄与管理信息。
Listed criteria include herd, birth year, sex, birth type, dam age, breed, measurement date, age, and management information.

具体组合随性状与评估设计变化，不能当作所有动物评估的统一编码表。
The combination varies with traits and evaluation design, rather than defining a universal coding scheme.

02 · 补充阅读：管理历史不能丢 / Supporting reading: retain management history

BREEDPLAN 记录手册的 PDF 第 44—46 页区分出生前与出生后的管理影响。
PDF pages 44–46 of the BREEDPLAN recording manual distinguish pre-birth and post-birth management influences.

它还说明，曾经分开管理的动物即使后来混群，早期差异仍可能影响后续分组。
It also explains that earlier separate management can affect later grouping even after animals are reunited.

因此“今天同栏”不等于“从出生起经历相同”。
Being housed together today does not establish identical histories since birth.

这条肉牛系统规则用于提醒保留历史，种猪如何建模仍需按具体测定方案决定。
This cattle-system rule motivates retaining history, while pig modeling still depends on its testing protocol.

03 · 教学例子：相同记录，不同背景 / Teaching example: identical records, different backgrounds

以下四头猪的数据完全是假设，单位均为 g/day。
The following four-pig dataset is wholly hypothetical, with all values in g/day.

  • 管理组 A 的两头猪分别为 800、900，均值为 850。
    Management group A contains records 800 and 900, with mean 850.

  • 管理组 B 的两头猪分别为 900、1,000，均值为 950。
    Management group B contains records 900 and 1,000, with mean 950.

两头记录为 900 的猪，原始表现相同，但组内偏差分别为 +50 与 −50。
The two pigs recorded at 900 have identical raw performance but within-group deviations of +50 and −50.

这说明比较背景会影响解释，却还不能证明 A 组那头猪的真实育种值更高。
This shows why comparison context matters without proving that the A-group pig has higher true breeding value.

两个组可能同时存在环境差异和遗传均值差异。
The groups may differ in both environment and mean genetic merit.

Purdue 遗传价值估计资料特别区分组内偏差与跨组遗传比较。
The Purdue genetic-merit resource distinguishes within-group deviations from across-group genetic comparisons.

不能把“减去组均值”直接改名为正式 EBV。
Subtracting a group mean does not create an official EBV.

04 · 把管理记录拆成事件 / Represent management as events

下面是本教程提出的记录结构，不是 BREEDPLAN 官方文件格式。
The structure below is an original recording proposal, not an official BREEDPLAN format.

animal_id, event_start, event_end, management_code, reason
P001, 2026-08-01, 2026-08-20, FEED_A, standard_feed
P002, 2026-08-01, 2026-08-20, FEED_B, different_feed
P002, 2026-08-21, 2026-08-25, HEALTH_X, treatment_event

日期、编号和事件都是假设，只用于说明应保留时间范围与原因。
Dates, IDs, and events are hypothetical and illustrate retaining time intervals and reasons.

最终同期群由性状定义、测定窗口、必要校正和真实管理经历共同决定。
Final contemporary groups depend on trait definitions, testing windows, required adjustments, and actual management histories.

不能看到父系或 EBV 后，再为了改变排名修改组别。
Do not redefine groups after seeing sire identity or EBVs merely to change rankings.

需要特别分清的两种错误 / Two different errors

  • 漏分组把显著不同待遇当成相同机会。
    Under-grouping treats substantially different management as equal opportunity.

  • 过分组把真正可比的动物拆得太碎。
    Over-grouping fragments animals that are genuinely comparable.

改进目标是保留真实比较，而不是一味增加或减少组数。
The goal is genuine comparison, not simply maximizing or minimizing group counts.

05 · 猪场如何借鉴 / Applying the principle to pigs

Purdue 记录规范把身份、测量质量与控制非遗传差异作为记录基础。
Purdue’s recording guidance treats identity, measurement quality, and control of non-genetic differences as recording foundations.

实际应先问数据是否同口径，再问模型能否合理解释年龄、性别、胎次或管理差异。
First check consistent definitions, then whether the model reasonably addresses age, sex, parity, or management differences.

这些因素可以采用分组、协变量或其他效应处理，并非每个字段都要机械拼成一个组号。
These factors may enter grouping, covariates, or other effects rather than being mechanically concatenated into one group ID.

同一个栏号需要连同管理历史看 / Read a Pen ID Alongside Management History

两头猪今天同场同栏，其中一头过去转过场并补过饲，我会先保留这些事件，再按性状和测定方案判断哪些要用于分组。
If two pigs share a pen today and one previously moved farms and received extra feed, I retain those events and then use the trait and testing protocol to decide what matters for grouping.
