阅读路线 / Reading Routes

For my learning route, read Chapters 01–09 in order, starting with the data, then evaluation, and finally how the results inform decisions.

方差为什么除以 n−1 / Why variance uses n−1

读 03 第04—06节的五步手算，再看四种抽样结果如何解释无偏修正。
Read the five-step calculation in Chapter 03, Sections 04–06, then see how four possible samples explain the unbiasedness correction.

想理解公猪为什么也有产仔数育种值，先读 02，再读 06。
To understand why a boar can have a litter-size EBV, read 02, then 06.

三种“关系”如何区分 / Distinguishing different relationships

遗传力与性状相关见 04，动物间亲缘关系见 05。
See 04 for heritability and trait correlations, and 05 for relationships between animals.

这些量描述不同对象，数值相同也不能互换。
These quantities describe different objects and are not interchangeable even when their numerical values match.

Read 06 and 07, focusing on environmental adjustment, information, and shrinkage.

同胞与基因组 / Siblings and genomics
