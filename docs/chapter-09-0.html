09-0｜选择、选配与遗传进展 / Selection, mating, and genetic progress

目录 / Contents · 上一章 / Previous · 术语速查 / Glossary

模型算出了育种值，接下来是不是按第一名选就行，得先看目标和可比较的对象。
Once a model produces breeding values, picking the top-ranked animal still depends on the objective and who can be compared.

我把评估、选择和选配当成三次业务交接来读，每一步的输入、输出和要核对的条件都不同。
I read evaluation, selection, and mating as three successive handoffs, each with its own inputs, outputs, and conditions to check.

我的笔记｜评估给出信息，选择决定留谁，选配决定怎样产生下一代。
My note | Evaluation provides information, selection chooses parents, and mating determines how the next generation is produced.

01 · 三个步骤分开理解 / Three distinct steps

  • 评估估计每头动物的遗传价值。
    Evaluation estimates each animal’s genetic merit.

  • 选择决定保留谁作为父母。
    Selection chooses which animals become parents.

  • 选配决定谁和谁配，以及各使用多少次。
    Mating determines pairs and parental contributions.

如果高 EBV 动物集中在一个家系，只按排名大量使用会增加群体亲缘和后续近交。
If high-EBV animals cluster in one family, using them heavily on rank alone increases population relationships and future inbreeding.

02 · 先定目标，再挑指标 / Define objectives before choosing criteria

育种目标是最终希望改进的综合结果。
The breeding objective is the overall outcome to improve.

选择依据是实际可用的记录、EBV 或标记。
Selection criteria are the available records, EBVs, or markers.

例如目标可以是整个生产系统的效益与稳健性，日增重只是依据之一。
For example, the objective may include system-wide efficiency and robustness, with daily gain as just one criterion.

终端父系与母系的育种目标通常不同。
Terminal-sire and maternal-line objectives commonly differ.

03 · 综合指数不是简单相加 / An index needs meaningful weights

把日增重的 g/day 与背膘的 mm 直接相加，没有统一的解释。
Adding daily gain in g/day directly to backfat in mm has no consistent interpretation.

简化综合育种目标可写为 Ĥ=∑jvjBV̂j\widehat H=\sum_j v_j\widehat{BV}_j。
A simplified aggregate breeding objective is Ĥ=∑jvjBV̂j\widehat H=\sum_j v_j\widehat{BV}_j.

其中 vjv_j 是每个性状单位对应的经济价值，或经过明确设计的权重。
Here vjv_j is economic value per trait unit or another explicitly designed weight.

它不同于用原始记录构建最优选择指数时的统计系数。
It differs from statistical coefficients in an optimal selection index built from raw information.

一个假设例子 / A hypothetical example

假设指数为 I=0.02EBVADG−1.0EBVBFI=0.02\,EBV_{ADG}-1.0\,EBV_{BF}。
Suppose the index is I=0.02EBVADG−1.0EBVBFI=0.02\,EBV_{ADG}-1.0\,EBV_{BF}.

  • 甲的两个 EBV 为 +40、+1，指数为 −0.2。
    Animal A has EBVs +40 and +1, giving an index of −0.2.

  • 乙的两个 EBV 为 +20、−1，指数为 1.4。
    Animal B has EBVs +20 and −1, giving an index of 1.4.

乙的综合得分更高，尽管它的日增重 EBV 较低。
B has the higher overall score despite its lower daily-gain EBV.

数值和权重都是教学假设，改变目标或权重就可能改变排名。
These values and weights are teaching assumptions, and changing the objective or weights can change rankings.

04 · 同期群让比较更公平 / Contemporary groups support fair comparisons

同期群把表现机会相近的动物放在一起比较，常涉及场、时间、性别和管理。
Contemporary groups compare animals with similar performance opportunities, often considering farm, time, sex, and management.

  • 分组太粗，容易混入环境差异。
    Broad groups can mix environmental differences.

  • 分组太细，会缺少可比较的动物。
    Overly fine groups can leave too few animals to compare.

具体规则依性状和系统设计而定，不能直接照搬其他物种的规则。
Specific rules depend on traits and evaluation design, so rules from another species should not be copied blindly.

遗传联系通过共同父系或其他亲属，在不同组之间建立桥梁。
Genetic connectedness bridges groups through shared sires or other relatives.

若每场只有一个完全不同且无亲缘的父系，就难以分开场区优势与父系优势。
If each farm has one entirely different, unrelated sire family, farm and sire advantages are difficult to distinguish.

共享父系并合理分布后代，有助于跨组比较。
Shared sires and appropriately distributed offspring support across-group comparisons.

05 · 遗传进展为什么按年看 / Why measure progress per year?

简化的每年遗传进展关系为 ΔGyear≈irA,ÂσA/L\Delta G_{year}\approx i\,r_{A,\hat A}\,\sigma_A/L。
A simplified annual-response relationship is ΔGyear≈irA,ÂσA/L\Delta G_{year}\approx i\,r_{A,\hat A}\,\sigma_A/L.

  • ii 是选择强度。
    ii is selection intensity.

  • rr 是选择准确度，不是两个性状的遗传相关。
    rr is selection accuracy, not a genetic correlation between two traits.

  • σA\sigma_A 是加性遗传标准差。
    σA\sigma_A is the additive genetic standard deviation.

  • LL 是世代间隔，即替代后代出生时父母的平均年龄。
    LL is the generation interval, the average parental age at birth of replacement offspring.

世代间隔不是妊娠长度。
Generation interval is not gestation length.

等待更多后代资料可能提高准确度，却也可能拉长世代间隔。
Waiting for more progeny data may improve accuracy while increasing the generation interval.

只用少数顶尖父母能增强选择，却可能损失多样性。
Using very few elite parents increases selection intensity but can reduce diversity.

因此要综合权衡准确度、时间和遗传多样性。
Accuracy, time, and genetic diversity therefore need to be balanced.

06 · 杂种优势与遗传趋势 / Heterosis and genetic trends

杂种优势是特定杂交群体相对亲本群体平均水平的优势。
Heterosis is the advantage of a particular crossbred population relative to the parental-population average.

它不能当作某头动物的加性 EBV。
It is not an individual’s additive EBV.

遗传趋势通常看同一可比基准下，不同出生年份的平均 EBV。
A genetic trend often tracks mean EBV by birth year on a comparable base.

生产均值上升可能包含管理改善，而改变基准或模型也可能改变表观趋势。
Production gains can include management improvements, while changed bases or models can alter apparent trends.

我的核对笔记 / My Verification Notes

用指数排序前，我会先确认两个 EBV 的单位、方向和评估基准可比，再检查权重对应的选择目标。
Before ranking by an index, I check the units, directions, and evaluation bases of the EBVs and verify which selection objective the weights represent.

前面的甲得 −0.2、乙得 1.4，只在例子给定的基准和权重下成立，换基准或目标就要重新核对。
The scores of −0.2 for A and 1.4 for B apply under the example’s base and weights, so changing the base or objective calls for a new check.

选择还要看哪些动物有资格进入名单，选配要继续看亲缘和用种次数，长期比较则要把世代间隔放进同一年度尺度。
Selection also asks which animals are eligible, mating considers relationships and parent usage, and long-term comparisons put generation intervals on the same annual scale.
