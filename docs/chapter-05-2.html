05-2｜亲缘、共祖与近交计算 / Relationship, kinship, and inbreeding calculations

目录 / Contents · 返回主文 / Main chapter

我把 A 元素、共祖和近交系数放在一张对照表里，代入同一个系谱之前先写明是在比两头动物，还是在算一头后代。
I keep A entries, kinship, and inbreeding in one reference table and identify whether a calculation compares two animals or describes one offspring before using the same pedigree.

原始来源是 MU Extension：Inbreeding: Its Meaning, Uses and Effects on Farm Animals，下面区分其标准化关系系数与动物模型常用 A 元素。
The original source is MU Extension: Inbreeding: Its Meaning, Uses and Effects on Farm Animals, with its normalized relationship distinguished below from animal-model A entries.

本篇于 2026-10-18 核对来源，来源要点采用概括，教学数据与演算均为自拟。
Sources were checked on 2026-10-18, source points are summarized, and teaching data and calculations are original.

我想弄懂的事｜父母关系为 0.5，为什么子代近交系数是 0.25？
What I want to understand | Why does a parental relationship of 0.5 give offspring inbreeding of 0.25?

01 · 来源要点与系数口径 / Source points and coefficient conventions

原文以共同祖先路径解释亲缘和近交，并指出后果随物种、性状和群体而变。
The source uses common-ancestor paths to explain relationship and inbreeding and notes that consequences vary by species, trait, and population.

其关系公式包含个体近交程度的标准化项，所以在近交动物间不能一概把原文 R 写成 A。
Its relationship formula includes normalization for individual inbreeding, so its R must not universally be equated with A for inbred animals.

02 · 三个量分别描述什么 / What do the three quantities describe?

  • A 元素 AijA_{ij}描述动物 i,ji,j 相对基准的加性遗传协方差比例。
    An A entry AijA_{ij} describes the additive genetic covariance between animals i,ji,j relative to the base variance.

  • 共祖系数 ϕij\phi_{ij}是从两头动物各随机取一个常染色体等位基因拷贝，两者同祖同源的概率。
    Kinship ϕij\phi_{ij} is the probability that autosomal allele copies sampled one from each animal are identical by descent.

  • 个体近交系数 FkF_k描述同一个体两个对应拷贝同祖同源的概率。
    Individual inbreeding FkF_k describes the probability that the corresponding two copies within one animal are identical by descent.

在相同基准和标准系谱定义下，Aij=2ϕijA_{ij}=2\phi_{ij}，而子代 FF 等于父母共祖。
Under the same base and standard pedigree definitions, Aij=2ϕijA_{ij}=2\phi_{ij} and offspring FF equals parental kinship.

FC=ϕS,D=AS,D2,ACC=1+FC F_C=\phi_{S,D}=\frac{A_{S,D}}{2},\qquad A_{CC}=1+F_C

03 · 先构建一个小 A 矩阵 / Build a small A matrix

假设基础动物 S、D 互不相关且不近交，C1、C2 都是它们的子代。
Assume founders S and D are unrelated and non-inbred, and C1 and C2 are offspring of both.

对父母先于后代排序的系谱，后代与较早动物 jj 的关系可递推为父母关系的平均。
With parents ordered before offspring, an offspring’s relationship to an earlier animal jj is recursively the average parental relationship.

Aij=12(Asi,j+Adi,j),Aii=1+12Asi,di A_{ij}=\tfrac12(A_{s_i,j}+A_{d_i,j}),\qquad A_{ii}=1+\tfrac12 A_{s_i,d_i}

先算 C1 与 S：(ASS+ADS)/2=(1+0)/2=0.5(A_{SS}+A_{DS})/2=(1+0)/2=0.5。
First calculate C1 with S: (ASS+ADS)/2=(1+0)/2=0.5(A_{SS}+A_{DS})/2=(1+0)/2=0.5.

再算 C2 与 C1：(AS,C1+AD,C1)/2=(0.5+0.5)/2=0.5(A_{S,C1}+A_{D,C1})/2=(0.5+0.5)/2=0.5。
Then calculate C2 with C1: (AS,C1+AD,C1)/2=(0.5+0.5)/2=0.5(A_{S,C1}+A_{D,C1})/2=(0.5+0.5)/2=0.5.

A=[100.50.5010.50.50.50.510.50.50.50.51]S,D,C1,C2 A=\begin{bmatrix} 1&0&0.5&0.5\\ 0&1&0.5&0.5\\ 0.5&0.5&1&0.5\\ 0.5&0.5&0.5&1 \end{bmatrix}_{S,D,C1,C2}

矩阵对角线为 1，是因为这四头在当前基础假设下都不近交，不是因为动物与自身“没有近交风险”。
The diagonal is 1 because these four animals are non-inbred under the stated base assumptions, not because self-relatedness means no inbreeding risk.

04 · 由父母关系计算子代 F / Calculate offspring F

若把全同胞 C1、C2 作为一对假设父母，它们子代的 F=0.5/2=0.25F=0.5/2=0.25。
If full siblings C1 and C2 are hypothetical parents, their offspring has F=0.5/2=0.25F=0.5/2=0.25.

这个子代的对角元为 1+0.25=1.251+0.25=1.25，而不是 0.25。
That offspring’s diagonal entry is 1+0.25=1.251+0.25=1.25, not 0.25.

若父母是无其他背景亲缘的半同胞，A=0.25A=0.25，所以子代 F=0.125F=0.125。
For half-sibling parents without additional background relationships, A=0.25A=0.25 and offspring F=0.125F=0.125.

这两例用于识别系数尺度，不构成推荐这种选配的建议。
These examples identify coefficient scales and do not recommend these matings.

05 · 标准化关系 R 为什么有时不等于 A / Why normalized R can differ from A

常见标准化加性关系定义为 Rij=Aij/AiiAjjR_{ij}=A_{ij}/\sqrt{A_{ii}A_{jj}}。
A common normalized additive relationship is Rij=Aij/AiiAjjR_{ij}=A_{ij}/\sqrt{A_{ii}A_{jj}}.

沿上例让 C1、C2 再产生两个全同胞 X、Y，则 X、Y 各有 F=0.25F=0.25，且 AXY=0.75A_{XY}=0.75。
Continuing the example, let C1 and C2 produce full siblings X and Y, each with F=0.25F=0.25 and with AXY=0.75A_{XY}=0.75.

这里 AC1,Y=(1+0.5)/2=0.75A_{C1,Y}=(1+0.5)/2=0.75，另一亲本相同，所以再取父母平均仍为 0.75。
Here AC1,Y=(1+0.5)/2=0.75A_{C1,Y}=(1+0.5)/2=0.75, with the same relationship for the other parent, so averaging the parental values again gives 0.75.

因此 RXY=0.75/1.25=0.6R_{XY}=0.75/1.25=0.6，共祖为 0.75/2=0.3750.75/2=0.375，不能把标准化 R 再除以 2 得到子代近交。
Therefore RXY=0.75/1.25=0.6R_{XY}=0.75/1.25=0.6 and kinship is 0.75/2=0.3750.75/2=0.375, so halving normalized R would not give offspring inbreeding.

06 · F 不是疾病概率 / F is not a disease probability

同祖同源 IBD指来自同一祖先拷贝，区别于仅观察到两个字母相同。
Identity by descent (IBD) means descent from one ancestral copy, unlike merely observing matching allele labels.

F=0.25F=0.25 不是有 25% 概率患病，也不表示 25% 的全部 DNA 序列相同。
F=0.25F=0.25 is neither a 25% disease probability nor a claim that 25% of the entire DNA sequence matches.

近交会增加同祖纯合的机会，可能带来隐性有害变异表达和近交衰退，但具体损失需要群体与性状证据。
Inbreeding increases autozygosity and can expose harmful recessives and cause inbreeding depression, but specific losses require population- and trait-specific evidence.

07 · 报表只写亲缘 0.25 时，先认尺度 / Identify the Scale of a Reported Relationship of 0.25

报表只写「亲缘 0.25」时，我会先查它表示 A 元素、共祖系数、标准化关系还是基因组指标，也会查参考基准。
When a report gives only “relationship 0.25,” I look up whether it is an A entry, kinship, normalized relationship, or a genomic measure, as well as its reference base.

在本例的标准系谱定义下，父母的 A 元素确认为 0.25 后，才能用 0.25/2=0.1250.25/2=0.125 得到子代近交系数。
Under the standard pedigree definition used here, I use 0.25/2=0.1250.25/2=0.125 for offspring inbreeding only after confirming the parents’ A entry is 0.25.
