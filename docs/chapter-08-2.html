08-2｜基因组质控与样本身份 / Genomic quality control and sample identity

目录 / Contents · 返回主文 / Main chapter

我把分型质控当作数据接入的一环，既看检出率，也核对样本和动物身份以及清洗前后的编号表。
I treat genotype QC as part of data ingestion, checking call rates, sample and animal identities, and ID lists before and after cleaning.

原始来源是 Masuda：Quality control of SNP markers，本篇解释质量指标与文件衔接，不执行真实样本剔除。
The original source is Masuda: Quality control of SNP markers, with this note explaining metrics and file alignment without filtering real samples.

本篇于 2026-10-18 核对来源，来源要点采用概括，教学数据与演算均为自拟。
Sources were checked on 2026-10-18, source points are summarized, and teaching data and calculations are original.

我想弄懂的事｜矩阵算得出来，是否就代表基因型和猪只身份正确？
What I want to understand | Does a computable matrix establish correct genotypes and animal identities?

01 · 来源要点 / Source points

原页区分位点和动物的质控，并强调清洗后的基因型与矩阵必须重新对齐。
The page distinguishes marker and animal QC and emphasizes realigning cleaned genotypes with relationship matrices.

作者建议将清洗文件、重建矩阵和评估分步处理，避免输出矩阵还保留旧编号造成混乱。
The author recommends separating cleaned-file creation, matrix rebuilding, and evaluation to avoid confusion from matrices retaining old IDs.

02 · 先定义 0、1、2 / Define 0, 1, and 2

设每个数表示该 SNP 上等位基因 B 的拷贝数，则 0=AA、1=AB、2=BB，缺失另行标记。
Let each number count B alleles at a SNP, so 0=AA, 1=AB, and 2=BB, with missingness coded separately.

0 不是差基因、2 不是好基因，编码方向与性状效应是不同问题。
Zero is not a bad genotype and two is not a good genotype, because coding direction differs from trait effect.

缺失不能随便填 0，否则会把未检测成功伪装成 AA。
Missing values must not be arbitrarily filled with zero, which would misrepresent failed detection as AA.

03 · 手算检出率 / Calculate call rates

假设有下面 3 头猪、4 个标记的教学数据，NA 表示缺失。
Suppose the teaching data below contain three pigs and four markers, with NA indicating missingness.

animal,M1,M2,M3,M4
P1,0,1,NA,2
P2,1,1,2,0
P3,2,0,1,NA

动物检出率是该动物成功检测的标记数除以计划标记数，所以 P1、P2、P3 分别为 0.75、1、0.75。
An animal call rate divides successfully called markers by planned markers, giving P1, P2, and P3 rates 0.75, 1, and 0.75.

标记检出率是该位点成功检测的动物数除以待检测动物数，所以 M3、M4 都为 2/32/3。
A marker call rate divides animals successfully called at that marker by animals tested, giving 2/32/3 for both M3 and M4.

例如选择阈值 0.8 时，P1、P3 与 M3、M4 会分别触及相应维度的阈值，但该数字仅为教学假设。
A hypothetical threshold of 0.8 flags P1, P3, M3, and M4 in their respective dimensions, but this threshold is illustrative only.

过滤动物会改变剩余位点统计，过滤位点也会改变动物统计，因此应记录步骤顺序、分母与最终保留集合。
Filtering animals changes marker statistics and filtering markers changes animal statistics, so record order, denominators, and final retained sets.

04 · 手算等位基因频率与 MAF / Calculate allele frequency and MAF

等位基因频率 pBp_B是 B 拷贝数除以成功检测个体提供的全部拷贝数。
Allele frequency pBp_B divides B-copy count by all allele copies from successfully called animals.

M3 的有效编码为 2、1，共有 3 个 B 拷贝和 4 个总拷贝。
The called M3 genotypes are 2 and 1, giving three B copies among four total copies.

pB=(2+1)/(2×2)=0.75,MAF=min⁡(pB,1−pB)=0.25 p_B=(2+1)/(2\times2)=0.75,\qquad MAF=\min(p_B,1-p_B)=0.25

MAF是次要等位基因频率，不是次要基因型所占动物比例。
MAF is minor allele frequency, not the fraction of animals with the less common genotype.

若一个标记在保留群体中完全没有变异，称为单态，通常不能提供该群体内的关系差异信息。
A marker with no variation in the retained population is monomorphic and generally supplies no within-population relationship contrast.

05 · 孟德尔一致性与样本身份 / Mendelian consistency and identity

在同一编码方向下，某已知亲本为 AA 时，正常孟德尔遗传下不能产生 BB 子代。
Under consistent coding, a known AA parent cannot produce a BB offspring through ordinary Mendelian inheritance.

出现大量这类矛盾时，要核对亲子身份、样本错配、等位基因方向和检测错误，而不是直接认定系谱真实或芯片真实。
Numerous such conflicts require checking parentage, sample swaps, allele orientation, and assay errors rather than automatically trusting either pedigree or chip.

极高相似度可能提示重复样本，但需要结合动物身份与真实生物学关系判断。
Extremely high similarity can suggest duplicate samples but requires interpretation with identities and biological relationships.

交叉编号表把样本编号、原始猪号、软件重编号和矩阵行号连接起来，是防止身份错位的关键。
A cross-reference table connects sample ID, original animal ID, software ID, and matrix row, helping prevent identity mismatches.

06 · HWE 检查怎样理解 / Interpret HWE checks

哈代–温伯格平衡 HWE是在特定随机交配等条件下，基因型频率与等位基因频率对应的一种基准。
Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium (HWE) is a baseline relating genotype and allele frequencies under conditions including random mating.

在二等位的基准下，AA、AB、BB 期望频率分别为 (1−pB)2(1-p_B)^2、2pB(1−pB)2p_B(1-p_B)、pB2p_B^2。
For a biallelic baseline, expected AA, AB, and BB frequencies are (1−pB)2(1-p_B)^2, 2pB(1−pB)2p_B(1-p_B), and pB2p_B^2.

偏离可能来自检测问题，也可能来自选择、群体混合或近交，不能在选育群体中把每个偏离都直接当作错误。
Deviations can reflect assay problems, selection, population mixture, or inbreeding, so not every deviation in a selected population is an error.

07 · 清洗后重新核对矩阵和编号 / Reconcile IDs and Matrices After Cleaning

删除一头低检出率动物后，我会核对矩阵实际保留的行列和顺序，也会查软件是否保留或填零。
After excluding an animal with a low call rate, I check the matrix’s retained rows, columns, and order, as well as whether the software retains or zero-fills entries.

新的编号表要与重建后的矩阵对齐，若沿用原矩阵，就按软件行为和清洗结果逐项建立映射。
A new ID file must match the rebuilt matrix, or if the existing matrix is reused, IDs must be mapped explicitly according to the software’s behavior and cleaning results.
