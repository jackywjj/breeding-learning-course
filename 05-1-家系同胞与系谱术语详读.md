# 05-1｜家系、同胞与系谱术语 / Families, siblings, and pedigree terms

[目录 / Contents](00-目录.md) · [返回主文 / Main chapter](05-0-家系系谱与近交.md)

遇到同窝、同父和同栏几个词，我会先找各自需要的字段，再决定能从中判断哪一种关系。  
When I read terms like littermates, paternal siblings, and penmates, I first identify the fields needed to establish each kind of relationship.

原始来源是 [MU Extension：Beef Production Glossary](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g2030)，原文对象为肉牛，本篇只转用通用遗传术语并使用自拟猪系谱。  
The original source is [MU Extension: Beef Production Glossary](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g2030), concerning beef cattle, while this note transfers only general genetic terms and uses an original pig pedigree.

本篇于 **2026-10-18** 核对来源，来源要点采用概括，教学数据与演算均为自拟。  
Sources were checked on **2026-10-18**, source points are summarized, and teaching data and calculations are original.

> **我想弄懂的事｜同父、同母、同窝和同栏，分别意味着什么？**  
> **What I want to understand | What is implied by sharing a sire, dam, litter, or pen?**

## 01 · 来源要点 / Source points

原文条目“Sibs”区分全同胞与父系、母系半同胞，系谱条目说明祖先身份如何用于遗传评估。  
The source entry “Sibs” distinguishes full and paternal or maternal half siblings, while the pedigree entry links ancestry to genetic evaluation.

本篇不沿用牛的称重日龄、繁殖阈值或 EPD 报表规范。  
This note does not transfer cattle weighing ages, reproductive thresholds, or EPD reporting rules.

## 02 · 先看最小系谱 / Read a minimal pedigree

下表记录动物、父亲、母亲，0 仅表示父母未知，不是一头真实的公共祖先。  
The table records animal, sire, and dam, with zero indicating unknown parentage rather than one real shared ancestor.

```csv
animal,sire,dam
S1,0,0
S2,0,0
D1,0,0
D2,0,0
A,S1,D1
B,S1,D1
C,S1,D2
D,S2,D1
```

- A 与 B **同父同母**，所以是全同胞。  
  A and B **share both parents** and are full siblings.

- A 与 C **同父异母**，所以是父系半同胞。  
  A and C **share a sire but have different dams** and are paternal half siblings.

- A 与 D **同母异父**，所以是母系半同胞。  
  A and D **share a dam but have different sires** and are maternal half siblings.

在常染色体加性模型中，两种半同胞关系在相同基础假设下具有相同的期望加性亲缘，但母体环境来源可能不同。  
In an autosomal additive model, both half-sibling types have the same expected additive relationship under matching base assumptions, while maternal environmental sources can differ.

## 03 · 同胞与同窝为什么不同 / Why siblings and littermates differ

**同窝**要求同一次分娩，因此还需要 `litter_id` 或母猪加分娩日期等事件信息。  
**Littermates** require one farrowing event, needing a `litter_id` or information such as sow and farrowing date.

同一对父母在不同胎次产生的后代可以是全同胞，但不是同窝。  
Offspring of the same two parents from different farrowings can be full siblings without being littermates.

**同栏**是共同饲养环境的标签，既可能包含亲属，也可能包含无近亲关系的猪。  
**Penmates** share a management environment and may be related or lack close relationships.

寄养还会使出生母猪与哺乳母猪不同，因此生物学母亲与养育母亲最好分别记录。  
Fostering can separate birth and nursing dams, so biological and rearing mothers should ideally be recorded separately.

## 04 · 四种字段回答四种问题 / Four fields answer four questions

- `sire_id, genetic_dam_id` 回答遗传父母是谁。  
  `sire_id, genetic_dam_id` identify genetic parents.

- `birth_litter_id` 回答出生属于哪个分娩事件。  
  `birth_litter_id` identifies the birth farrowing event.

- `foster_dam_id` 回答由哪头母猪哺育。  
  `foster_dam_id` identifies the nursing dam.

- `pen_id` 加日期区间回答何时在哪一栏饲养。  
  `pen_id` plus dates identifies when and where an animal was housed.

这些字段不能互相替代，否则模型可能把共同环境误归入遗传。  
These fields are not interchangeable, or the model may attribute shared environment to genetics.

## 05 · 家系平均为何不足 / Why a family average is insufficient

假设 A 家系平均 900、B 家系平均 850 g/day，但 A 家系全部在更好的测定环境。  
Suppose Family A averages 900 and Family B 850 g/day, but every A animal has a better testing environment.

若环境优势估计为 80，则按这个教学校正，A 的比较值为 $900-80=820$，原始家系排名可能反转。  
If the environmental advantage is estimated as 80, this teaching adjustment gives A $900-80=820$, potentially reversing the raw family ranking.

80 必须来自有依据的估计，不能因为想改变结果而临时指定。  
The 80 must come from justified estimation and must not be assigned merely to change the result.

动物模型还会利用不同动物间的亲缘和信息量，而不只对家系均值做简单扣减。  
An animal model also uses animal relationships and information amounts rather than merely subtracting an adjustment from family means.

## 06 · 系谱作为数据库如何检查 / Validate pedigree as a database

动物编号应唯一，父母必须能在系谱或明确的未知规则中解析，且不允许自身成为祖先。  
Animal IDs must be unique, parents must resolve within the pedigree or an explicit unknown-parent rule, and an animal cannot be its own ancestor.

父母与后代日期矛盾、循环关系和导入重复必须先核对，不能依赖评估软件替你解释真实身份。  
Conflicting parent–offspring dates, cycles, and duplicate imports require checking before expecting evaluation software to determine real identities.

## 07 · 未知父亲码在表里怎么处理 / Handling Unknown Sires in a Pedigree Table

两个父号都记作 0 时，我把它们当作两条缺少父亲身份的记录来处理，单凭这个标记不能识别父系半同胞。  
I treat two sire IDs coded zero as two records with unknown sires because the missing value alone cannot identify paternal half siblings.
